Home water

Percy Priest Lake.

Just east of downtown Nashville, Percy Priest is one of Middle Tennessee’s best bass lakes, and it’s Captain Cory’s home water. He fishes it year-round, so he knows where the fish are and where they’re headed next.

Bass species

01

The water

Rocky points, bluff walls, standing timber, creek channels and offshore humps. Plenty of places for big bass to hide, and live sonar shows us exactly where they are.

02

The fish

Largemouth, smallmouth and spotted bass, plus the occasional striped bass. Some days you’ll catch all three black bass before lunch.

03

The seasons

Shallow spawners in spring, offshore schools in summer and fall, and slow, heavy fish in winter. Your captain runs the pattern that’s working right now.

Season by season

When to go.

Bass are catchable on Percy Priest all year. The pattern changes with the seasons, and we fish whatever is working right now.

01

Spring

Bass move shallow to feed and spawn. Great time for numbers and big females on the banks and flats.

02

Summer

Fish group up offshore on ledges, humps and points. Live sonar shines here: we find the school, then go to work.

03

Fall

Bass chase bait into the creeks and pockets. Fast, fun fishing when the shad move.

04

Winter

Slower, deeper fish, but some of the heaviest of the year. Patient, precise fishing pays off.
