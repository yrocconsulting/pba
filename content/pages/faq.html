Good to know

Before you go.

I’ve never bass fished. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Captain Cory coaches every angler from the first cast: how to work the bait, feel the bite and set the hook. Plenty of first-timers leave with their best fish ever.

Can you take a large group?

Yes. Each boat fishes 1 or 2 anglers. For larger parties, Captain John Boy runs a second boat alongside Captain Cory, priced per boat. Put your party size on the booking form or call or text (615) 310-1058 to plan it.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Kids are welcome, and some of our favorite catches have come from young anglers. A half-day trip is a great fit for younger kids.

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes. Tennessee requires a fishing license for anglers 13 and older. You can buy one online from the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency (TWRA) in a few minutes.

What should I bring?

Your license, sunglasses, sunscreen, layers or rain gear for the season, and food and drinks for the day. Rods, reels and tackle are on the boat.

Where do we meet?

Trips launch from a boat ramp on Percy Priest Lake. Cory sends the exact ramp and start time when he confirms your trip.

How much does a trip cost?

A half-day guided trip is $550 and a full-day guided trip is $950. Prices are per boat, for 1 or 2 anglers. A $150 deposit secures your date, for either trip.

How do I book?

Send a request with the form below or call or text (615) 310-1058. Once we confirm your date, a $150 deposit locks it in.

How long are the trips?

A half day is about 4 hours, morning or afternoon. A full day is about 8 hours with a sunrise start.

What’s included?

A rigged bass boat with live forward-facing sonar, rods, reels and tackle, and photos of your catch. Bring your license, sunglasses, layers and food and drinks.

Do I need experience?

No. Trips are built around your skill level, from first-timers and kids to tournament anglers looking to learn live sonar.
