Home water

Percy Priest Lake.

Just east of downtown Nashville, Percy Priest is one of Middle Tennessee’s best bass lakes, and it’s Cory’s home water. He fishes it year-round, so he knows where the fish are and where they’re headed next.

Bass species

01

The water

Rocky points, bluff walls, standing timber, creek channels and offshore humps. Plenty of places for big bass to hide, and live sonar shows us exactly where they are.

02

The fish

Largemouth, smallmouth and spotted bass. Some days you’ll catch all three before lunch.

03

The seasons

Shallow spawners in spring, offshore schools in summer and fall, and slow, heavy fish in winter. Cory runs the pattern that’s working right now.

Good to know

Before you go.

I’ve never bass fished. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Cory coaches every angler from the first cast: how to work the bait, feel the bite and set the hook. Plenty of first-timers leave with their best fish ever.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Kids are welcome, and some of our favorite catches have come from young anglers. A half-day trip is a great fit for younger kids.

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes. Tennessee requires a fishing license for anglers 13 and older. You can buy one online from the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency (TWRA) in a few minutes.

What should I bring?

Your license, sunglasses, sunscreen, layers or rain gear for the season, and food and drinks for the day. Rods, reels and tackle are on the boat.

Where do we meet?

Trips launch from a boat ramp on Percy Priest Lake. Cory sends the exact ramp and start time when he confirms your trip.

How do I book?
